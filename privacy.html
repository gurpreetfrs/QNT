Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Qualitant is designed to be private. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it never sells or shares your information.

Your data stays on your device

Your quotes, timetable, notes and reminders are stored on your device. The one exception is the optional Lock Screen card, described below, and even then the server can't read them.

Live Activity updates (optional)

When you turn on the Live Activity, Qualitant uses a small server to keep the Lock Screen card up to date while the app is closed. To do this, your device sends the server:

  • Encrypted card contents. Before anything leaves your device, the upcoming cards (your quote, and the titles and times of upcoming classes and reminders) are encrypted with a key that is created on and never leaves your device. The server only stores and forwards this encrypted data. Neither I nor the hosting provider can read it.
  • When each card should appear. The server needs to know the times to send updates, but not what they contain.
  • Technical identifiers: a random ID created when you turn the feature on (not linked to your name, Apple Account or anything else about you), and push tokens issued by Apple that let the server send updates to this app on your device.

Each card is deleted from the server once it has been delivered. Turning the Live Activity off deletes everything the server holds about your device straight away, and if your device hasn't been in touch for 14 days, it is deleted automatically. The server runs on Cloudflare, and updates are delivered through Apple's Push Notification service. All connections are encrypted (HTTPS).

Calendar access

If you choose “Import from Calendar”, Qualitant asks for permission to read your calendar so you can copy events into your timetable. Calendar data is read on your device only, is never uploaded, and Qualitant never changes your calendar. You can turn this off at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Calendars.

Notifications

Class and reminder notifications are scheduled on your device and are not sent through any server.

Widgets

The Up Next widget displays information from the app on your device only.

Children

Qualitant does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes
