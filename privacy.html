Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

QNT is designed to be private. It does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information.

Your data stays on your device

Your quotes, timetable, notes and reminders are stored only on your device. They are never sent to me or to anyone else. QNT has no account system, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Calendar access

If you choose “Import from Calendar”, QNT asks for permission to read your calendar so you can copy events into your timetable. Calendar data is read on your device only, is never uploaded, and QNT never changes your calendar. You can turn this off at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Calendars.

Notifications

QNT uses notifications to remind you about your classes and reminders. These are scheduled on your device and are not sent through any server.

Live Activities and widgets

The Dynamic Island, Lock Screen card and Up Next widget display information from the app on your device only.

Children

QNT does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
