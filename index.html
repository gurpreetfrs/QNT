Common questions

How do I move between pages?
Pull down past the top, or up past the bottom, of any page.

How do I show my quote in the Dynamic Island?
On the Quote page, tap “Start Live Activity”. Use Options to choose what the Lock Screen card shows.

The Lock Screen card says “Information may be out of date”.
Tap the card to open QNT and it will refresh straight away.

I'm not getting reminders.
Check that notifications are allowed for QNT in Settings → Notifications → QNT.

Will I lose my data if I delete the app?
Yes. Everything is stored only on your device, so deleting QNT removes your quotes, timetable and notes.
